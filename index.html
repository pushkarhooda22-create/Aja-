About Aja

Welcome to Aja World Kitchen & Bar, where culinary excellence meets world-class hospitality. Located in the heart of Chandigarh, we bring together the finest flavors from around the globe in a sophisticated and welcoming atmosphere.

Our expert chefs craft innovative dishes using premium ingredients, while our skilled bartenders create signature cocktails that will delight your palate. Whether you're celebrating a special occasion or enjoying a casual dinner, Aja is your perfect destination.
